## 9. Insights — `insights.md`

1. Based on the output fo Q16 and Q33, Mobile Phones and Computing are the top-performing categories by total revenue (generating approximately 6.94 million and 6.60 million, respectively) and drive the highest overall profit amounts. However, Accessories stand out with the highest profitability efficiency by far, boasting an exceptional profit margin of 42.39% despite having the lowest total revenue and cost among all categories.
**Recommendation:** Since Accessories yield an exceptionally high profit margin of 42.39%, aggressively cross-sell and bundle them with high-volume purchases in Mobile Phones and Computing. Capitalizing on their high volume to push high-margin accessories will significantly boost overall profitability.

2. Based on the output of Q25, Nairobi overwhelmingly dominates regional sales performance at 9.58 million, generating more revenue than the next three top-performing cities—Mombasa (3.16M), Nakuru (2.39M), and Kiambu (1.65M)—combined. While the top tier is concentrated in Nairobi, secondary hubs like Mombasa and Nakuru display strong adoption, whereas lower-tier markets like Nyeri and Machakos drop below the 1 million mark, indicating that business growth is heavily centered in primary urban centers.
**Recommendation:** Focus Marketing and Fulfillment Expansion on Mombasa & Nakuru: Prioritize inventory allocation, targeted regional ad campaigns, and localized fulfillment infrastructure in Mombasa and Nakuru to capture the next wave of scaling growth outside of the saturated Nairobi hub.

3. Base on the output of Q27, Average customer expenditure scales clearly across tiers, led by VIP accounts with an average spend of 253,251.88, which is approximately 28% higher than Corporate accounts (197,958.71) and 55% higher than Retail buyers (163,575.21). This highlights that while retail accounts form the baseline spending volume, VIP and corporate clients deliver significantly greater financial density per customer.
**Recommendations:** Establish dedicated account management and premium service perks for the VIP and Corporate segments to protect high-yield accounts, while introducing tiered volume incentives to encourage top-spending Retail customers to transition into higher expenditure brackets.

4. Base on the output of Q34, A significant group of customers is showing clear signs of churn or extended dormancy, with 47 total accounts having gone anywhere from roughly 94 days (e.g., Wafula Korir) to over 600 days (e.g., Agnes Kimani at 625 days) without placing a new order.
**Recommendations:** Launch an immediate targeted win-back email or SMS campaign featuring personalized reactivation discounts or incentives tailored to these dormant accounts, prioritizing those in the 90-to-150-day window before they transition into complete churn.

5. Based on the output of Q40, Monthly revenue displays high volatility characterized by pronounced seasonal surges followed by sharp contractions—notably peaking in December 2025 (2.02M), June 2026 (2.03M), and August 2026 (2.24M)—while frequently dropping in the subsequent months (such as January 2026's -84.09% drop and May 2026's -28.79% dip). This pattern indicates that business performance is heavily dependent on periodic peak sales cycles rather than steady, predictable month-over-month compounding growth.
**Recommendations:** Build a robust mid-cycle retention and promotional calendar to bridge the revenue gaps during historical post-peak downturn (such as January and May), smoothing out cash flow volatility between major seasonal surges.
 
6. Based on the output of Q45, The customer base exhibits extreme revenue stratification across its 132 total accounts, where top-tier VIP buyers generate substantial lifetime expenditures scaling up to over 848,000, while a trailing low-value segment tapers off significantly down to under 2,000. This heavy reliance on a high-value core indicates that revenue is heavily concentrated among elite spenders rather than evenly distributed across the entire customer roster.
**Recommendations:** 
Implement a dedicated loyalty and concierge retention program to safeguard relationships with the "High Value (VIP)" segment, while deploying targeted automated marketing campaigns designed to upsell "Medium Value" and "Low Value" accounts into higher spending tiers. 
Instead of routing these valuable accounts through generic support channels, assign dedicated account managers or specialists to offer personalized assistance, priority ordering, exclusive perks, and proactive relationship management to prevent churn and keep top spenders loyal.
